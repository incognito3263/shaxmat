# CHESS NEXUS

A professional, real-time social chess platform built with FastAPI and React.

## Features
- ♟️ **Dynamic Gameplay**: Play vs AI or other players.
- 🌍 **Global Matchmaking**: Find opponents in real-time.
- 🤝 **Friend System**: Follow players and send friend requests.
- 📺 **Spectator Mode**: Watch live games as they happen.
- 💬 **In-game Chat**: Communicate with your opponent.
- 🏅 **Titles & Ranking**: Climb the leaderboard from Novice to Chess King.
- 🎨 **Board Themes**: Choose from 6 different professional themes.
- 📸 **Custom Avatars**: Upload your own photo or choose an emoji.
- 📜 **Game Review**: Analyze your past games move-by-move.

## Tech Stack
- **Backend**: Python, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, WebSockets, JWT.
- **Frontend**: React, TypeScript, Zustand, Framer Motion, TailwindCSS.
- **Database**: SQLite.
